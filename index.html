❓ Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions regarding orders, payments, delivery, and guarantees.

1. Website par toy order kaise place karein? (How to place an order?)

Sirf apna pasandeeda toy choose karein, 'Add to Basket' par click karein, phir 'Basket' drawer khol kar 'Checkout' button dabayein. Apna naam, city, delivery address aur mobile number enter karein. Aapka order foran book ho jayega aur aapko tracking code mil jayega!

2. Delivery charges aur Free Delivery ka kya rule hai?

Tamam Pakistan mein standard courier shipping sirf Rs. 250 hai. Lekin agar aapka order Rs. 5,999 ya us se zyada ka ho to Delivery bilkul FREE hai!

3. Payment ke kon se tareeqay available hain? (Payment Methods)

Aap delivery boy ko Cash on Delivery (COD) par cash pay kar sakte hain. Ya phir direct JazzCash / Easypaisa par advance payment bhej sakte hain (Account Title: Muhammad Jameel). Advance payment karne par aap checkout page par apni payment slip/screenshot bhi attach kar sakte hain!

4. Delivery kitne dino mein hoti hai? (Shipping Time)

Lahore mein 1 se 2 working days ke andar, aur baqi tamam cities (Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Multan, Faisalabad, Quetta, Gujranwala, Sialkot, etc.) mein 2 se 4 working days ke andar safe parcel deliver ho jata hai.

5. Agar khilouna damage ho to kya return ya exchange ho sakta hai?

Jee bilkul! MEHAR TOYS 7-Day Replacement Guarantee deta hai. Agar courier delivery ke doran koi piece break ho ya defected ho to foran hamare WhatsApp icon par click karke photo bhejein, hum bila-muawaza foran replacement parcel bhejenge!
